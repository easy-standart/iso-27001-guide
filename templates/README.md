## How to use

1. **Copy** the file you need into your repository or knowledge system (Confluence, Notion, SharePoint).
2. **Fill in** the placeholders in `[square brackets]` for your organization.
3. **Adapt** the italic comments in blockquote blocks (`> *text*`), or remove them before approval.
4. **Review** with responsible parties and get top management approval.
5. **Publish** in your corporate document system with version, date and signature.

Notation (same across all templates):

- Text in `[square brackets]` – placeholders that must be filled in.
- Italic text in blockquote (`> *...*`) – examples and drafter notes. Remove after adaptation.

## Important context

**This is a starter frame, not a final document.** An ISMS implementation project requires adapting templates to your business – industry, regulation, sector specifics, size, geography. Copying templates blindly is the most common cause of major NCs at Stage 2.

**Working order.**
- Start with the ISMS Scope Statement (file 02). It defines what's in scope.
- Then Risk Methodology (03) and IS Policy (01) – the foundation.
- Then SoA (04) – the link to Annex A.
- Topical policies (05, 06, 07) – as they become ready.

The full phased order is in [implementation-guide.md](../implementation-guide.md).

## What this doesn't cover

The starter set has 7 key documents out of the ~20 "readable" artefacts auditors expect to see at Stage 2. Not covered here (but you'll need them in your project):

- Cryptography and key management policy.
- Backup policy.
- Information classification and labelling policy.
- Asset management policy.
- Physical security policy.
- Mobile devices and remote work policy.
- Secure software development policy.
- Change management policy.
- Risk register (populated by the methodology in file 03).
- Risk treatment plan (RTP).
- Internal audit programme.
- Staff competence and training matrix.
- Legal requirements register.

The full list of what auditors actually look at is in [../implementation-guide.md](../implementation-guide.md) and [../checklist.md](../checklist.md).
