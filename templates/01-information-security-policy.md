## 1. Purpose

This Policy defines the strategic objectives, key principles and general rules for managing information security (IS) in the organisation. Developed in accordance with clause 5.2 of ISO/IEC 27001:2022.

The Policy serves as:

- the foundation for building an Information Security Management System (ISMS);
- a document demonstrating top management's commitment to IS;
- a tool for raising staff awareness;
- a signal to clients and partners that the organisation takes information protection seriously.

> *This section can be kept as-is or adapted to your organisation's specifics. The main thing is to clearly state that the Policy is a top-level strategic document.*

## 4. Key principles (CIA triad)

Information protection is built on preserving three fundamental properties defined in ISO/IEC 27000:

**Confidentiality.** [Access to information is limited to authorised persons and systems. The organisation protects data from unauthorised disclosure.]

**Integrity.** [Information is accurate, complete and protected from unauthorised modification or deletion. Any changes must be authorised.]

**Availability.** [Information and services are accessible to authorised users when needed. The organisation ensures continuity of business processes.]

> *The CIA triad is the basis of any IS system. Make sure these principles are clear to everyone on staff. You can add examples from your own operations if useful.*

## 7. Policy hierarchy

This Policy is a Level 1 document – the foundation for all topical IS policies and procedures. The ISMS document hierarchy is:

**Level 1 – high-level Information Security Policy.** Strategic document approved by top management.

**Level 2 – topical policies and procedures.** Detailed rules for specific IS areas:

- Asset management policy;
- Access control and password policy;
- Cryptography and key management policy;
- Physical security policy;
- Clear desk and clear screen policy;
- Secure software development policy;
- Mobile devices and remote work policy;
- Supplier and cloud services policy;
- Backup and recovery policy;
- Incident management procedure.

**Level 3 – work instructions and operational forms.** Detailed instructions for specific operations, logs, checklists.

> *Make sure all topical policies reference this top-level Policy. Create a visual hierarchy diagram to help staff understand it.*

## 9. Continual improvement

The organisation follows the Continual Improvement philosophy through the PDCA (Plan-Do-Check-Act) cycle:

- **Plan.** Setting objectives, assessing risks, developing procedures.
- **Do.** Implementing controls, training staff.
- **Check.** Monitoring, internal audits, incident analysis.
- **Act.** Corrective actions, documentation updates, risk reassessment.

**Continual improvement mechanisms:**

- Annual Management Review.
- Internal ISMS audits (at least annually).
- Regular reassessment of information security risks.
- Incident analysis and lessons learned.
- Monitoring of technology and threat landscape changes.

---

## 10. Compliance and sanctions

Compliance with this Policy and related procedures is mandatory for all staff.

Violation of IS requirements is considered a breach of work discipline and may result in:

- Verbal or written warning.
- Disciplinary action.
- Suspension of system access.
- Termination of employment.
- Referral to law enforcement (for illegal actions).

> *All incidents are documented and investigated per the Incident Management Procedure. Every employee is personally responsible for compliance.*
