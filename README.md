# ISO/IEC 27001 – a working guide for executives

> A practical reference for CISOs, compliance leads and process owners. Built around the realities of Kazakh businesses scaling into the EU, UK, UAE and Türkiye.

A quick orientation if this is your first visit. ISO/IEC 27001 is the international standard for information security management systems. Companies get certified against it. One certificate replaces dozens of security questionnaires in B2B tenders and covers 60–70% of the security-side requirements of GDPR, NIS2 and DORA.

This repository is not yet another collection of English-language templates. It is practical material focused on what the standard demands, what it costs, the right sequence to implement it, the mistakes that sink projects at Stage 2, and how the certificate turns into real revenue.

## How to use this repository

Pick the scenario that fits you.

**You are a CEO trying to decide whether your company needs this.** Start with [ceo-brief.md](ceo-brief.md). Then scan [verticals.md](verticals.md) to see your sector, and [comparison-regulations.md](comparison-regulations.md) to understand which laws you'll cover with a single certification.

**You are a Head of Compliance or CISO launching the project.** Start with [structure.md](structure.md) and [glossary.md](glossary.md). Then walk through [implementation-guide.md](implementation-guide.md) step by step. Come back to [annex-a-guide.md](annex-a-guide.md) and [checklist.md](checklist.md) as you progress through the phases.

**You are preparing for Stage 2.** Go straight to [checklist.md](checklist.md) and [annex-a-guide.md](annex-a-guide.md). The Stage 2 findings section will tell you which areas to double-check before the auditor arrives.

**You are buying consulting services and want to talk to vendors on equal terms.** Read [structure.md](structure.md), [glossary.md](glossary.md) and [ceo-brief.md](ceo-brief.md). That's enough to keep the conversation grounded and avoid paying for "full implementation" that turns out to be just a pile of templates.

## Who this is for

The repository targets executives who don't dig into the technical details of information security but make decisions about budget and priorities. If you're an engineer hunting for YAML-style checklists – there are hundreds of those on GitHub. This one is different: the language of business, regional context, and connections to regulation in Kazakhstan, the EU, UK, UAE and Türkiye.

The content is useful if you are:

- Preparing a tender with an enterprise client and running into the "ISO 27001 certificate" requirement.
- Considering expansion into the EU, UK or the Gulf and mapping the regulatory landscape.
- Weighing certification you've been "going to do for years" and finally want to assess the real cost.
- Preparing for Stage 2 after passing Stage 1 and want to double-check what might be missing.
- Working in finance, healthcare, SaaS or critical infrastructure, where certification is no longer optional.

**Consciously deferred to future iterations:**
